Dynamic Programming: Rod Cutting and Matrix-Chain Multiplication

Dynamic programming solves a problem from the answers to smaller overlapping subproblems, using optimal substructure: an optimal solution is built from optimal solutions to its subproblems. Each subproblem is solved once and stored, either top-down (recursion with memoization) or bottom-up (filling a table from small to large).
