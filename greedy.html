Greedy Algorithms: Activity Selection and Huffman Codes

A greedy algorithm builds its answer in small steps. At each step it takes the choice that looks best right now and never goes back on it. That makes it fast, but it is only correct when the problem has the greedy-choice property and optimal substructure. Try each problem, then switch strategies or examples to see when greedy fails.
