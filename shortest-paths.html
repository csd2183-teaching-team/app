Shortest Paths (Single-Source): Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford and A*

Each algorithm keeps a shortest-path estimate v.d and a predecessor v.π, and improves them with RELAX. They differ in which edges they relax and in what order. Dijkstra greedily takes the closest unfinished vertex using a priority queue and needs non-negative weights. Bellman-Ford relaxes every edge |V| − 1 times, allows negative weights and detects negative cycles. A* is Dijkstra aimed at one target, guided by an estimate h(v) of the distance still to go.
